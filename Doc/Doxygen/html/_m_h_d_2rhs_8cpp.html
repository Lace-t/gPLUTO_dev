Detailed Description

This function constructs the one-dimensional right hand sides of the conservative MHD or HD equations in the direction given by dir in different geometries. The right hand side in the d direction is computed as a two-point flux difference term plus a source term:

\[ R_{\bf i}^{(d)} = -\frac{\Delta t}{\Delta V_{\bf i}}\ \Big[ (AF)_{{\bf i} + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{\hat e}_d} -(AF)_{{\bf i} - \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{\hat e}_d} \Big] + \Delta t S_{\bf i} \]

where \({\bf i} = (i,j,k)\) while

  • \( A \): interface areas
  • \( {V} \): cell volume
  • \( F \): interface fluxes
  • \( \Delta t \): time step
  • \( S \): source term including geometrical terms and body forces.

See also the RHS_page.
